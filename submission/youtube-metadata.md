## Title

HawkerBridge | When More Budget Does Not Mean More Meals | DAISI Singapore 2026

## Description

A hawker closure notice tells a community team when a centre will close. HawkerBridge helps them decide what to investigate and what temporary support is feasible.

This demonstration follows 28 September 2026: 17 scheduled centre closures, six subzones flagged by a proximity screen, and a practical budget decision. With the stated assumptions, S$1,500 produces 225 planned meals. S$3,000 produces 450. At S$6,000, capacity still limits the proposal to 450 meals, with S$2,700 allocated.

Compare hypothetical cleaning changes, inspect cost and uptake assumptions, generate a constrained allocation, and save a reviewable proposal with PDF, CSV and JSON exports. Proposed localities still require an operator's verification.

Try HawkerBridge: https://hawkerbridge-sg.web.app
Code, data provenance and setup: https://github.com/shi1720/DAISI
Project owner: Shivam Gupta
Challenge: Databricks AI Social Impact Challenge, Singapore 2026
Problem statement: C3, KopilamAI

The actual Databricks pipeline completed successfully, published governed Delta tables, passed twelve quality checks and recorded nine scenario comparisons in MLflow. Three tabular sources were fetched live; two URA geometry archives were reused with their original dates and verified hashes. The public application uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and a Python API on Cloud Run. A Firebase recording is not a recording of a Databricks App.

The nine cloud cases cover Sep 22, Oct 5 and Dec 14 at S$600/S$1,500/S$3,000. The separate local robustness benchmark contains 15 runs, including the Sep 28 demonstration date and reach sensitivity. The displayed Sep 28 proposals are recomputed from the published data. Inspect Evidence & methods for the publication associated with the displayed results.

These are planning scenarios, not delivered meals or observed social impact. Census 2020 area totals are not current beneficiary counts. Distances use subzone representative points and straight lines, not walking routes. National waste figures do not establish centre-level food waste.

Data: NEA, SingStat and URA datasets from data.gov.sg. The repository preserves source identifiers, dates and checksums. HawkerBridge is an independent challenge project, not an agency-endorsed service.

Shivam Gupta initiated the brief, set the product goals and owns the project. Research, implementation, testing and presentation preparation used AI assistance.
